# Appwin Automate

Automations the person describes in plain words, run on their phone by themselves (in the
spirit of Tasker, without its screens).

- One place to ask: Change this app. Each message either adds or changes an automation, or changes
  the app itself.
- Each automation is a small script the agent writes (`automations/`), fenced to the automation
  API. A new or changed one arrives as a quick update, no install.
- Safety rails: a card to approve before an automation turns on (and again after it changes),
  asking before anything is sent, a run log, Stop all, and hard limits. Settings, Advanced, can
  turn the safety locks off (off by default, with a warning).
- Powers in this version: times and Ajman prayer times, speak and notify, Do Not Disturb. Planned:
  Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and charging, notifications from other apps, location, the app in front,
  Accessibility, Shizuku.
- Look: warm paper, graphite, one bottle green accent; the home screen shows today's prayer times.
