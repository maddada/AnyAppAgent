A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your first kiwi.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Describe what you want in everyday language. Kiwimake’s AI agent writes and builds the app, and you ask for changes in the same conversation.

Do I need a computer?

Yes, for the version available today. Install the Windows companion and keep your PC running while it makes or changes your apps. A cloud version is coming soon.

What do I need to get started?

An Android phone, a Windows PC and an AI connection set up in the desktop companion. The companion guides you through setup and pairing. Your AI provider’s usage or subscription costs may apply.

Why is the Android download an APK?

Kiwimake installs directly on your phone rather than through an app store. Open the downloaded APK and follow Android’s installation prompt. Apps you make arrive in Kiwimake for you to install the same way.

Can I change an app after making it?

Yes. Open its conversation in Kiwimake and describe the change. Your PC builds the update and sends it to your phone, ready to replace the previous version.

Can I use Kiwimake on iPhone or Mac?

This first version is for Android phones with a Windows companion. The downloads above are for those platforms.
